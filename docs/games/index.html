Complete gameFlipper Fray

A five-player pinball brawl: one round table, five pockets, a pair of flippers guarding each. Balls drift outward, bumpers and slingshots throw them around, and every ball that gets past your flippers costs you a life — five lives, last pocket standing wins, or the most lives when three minutes run out. You hold the bottom pocket against four AI keepers; multiball drops extra balls on a schedule and a bomb ball rolls out every half minute — two lives if it drains, a blast if nobody swallows it. ← → / A D flip, SPACE both; on touch, hold the left or right half of the screen. Physics: the flippers are kinematic bodies driven with setVelocityFromTarget, so a slap carries real angular speed into the ball; balls are bullet-flagged circles, so nothing tunnels through the thin ring walls; bumper and slingshot kicks and goal credit come through InteractionListeners. In 3D a neon arena with chrome balls, glowing pockets and lane lights, seen from behind your own pocket.

Complete gameSwarm Night

A survivor roguelite in the Vampire Survivors mould: five minutes in a walled graveyard, and you only steer — every weapon fires on its own. Kills drop XP gems; a full bar pauses the night for three upgrade cards drawn from six weapons (whip, wand, knives, ward, orbs, lightning) and eight passives. Six monster types arrive on a schedule, plus charging grave knights that carry treasure and a bone colossus at 4:00 whose slam throws the whole crowd. Dawn at 5:00 is the win. WASD / arrows to move, 1 / 2 / 3 or click to pick a card; on touch, hold anywhere to steer. Physics: up to 420 monsters are dynamic circles in a zero-gravity Space, so the horde crowds and streams around the stones with no flocking code; knockback is a mass-scaled impulse, and bolts, knives, spit and orbs are sensor bodies delivered through InteractionListeners with sensor-mask side filtering. In 3D a moonlit graveyard where every monster is an instanced low-poly rig.

Complete gameTin Legion

A lane-siege army builder on one large battlefield: gold trickles in, five troop cards wait at the bottom of the screen, and each one you play drops a squad of tin figurines that marches off on its own. You never steer a soldier — you choose what to field, where to drop it and when; hold a card and the road that squad would take is drawn on the ground, and the side of your gate you drop on picks its lane. Three roads run between the two keeps, a watchtower guards each centre road, and two gold mines sit on the flanks behind neutral camps. A captured mine pays gold and pushes your drop zone forward; a keep is shielded until its own watchtower falls. Ten cards, a free commander, and three difficulty levels for the opposing one. Physics: every figurine is a dynamic circle in a zero-gravity Space, so crowds shove and flow for free; shot is a sensor body delivered by InteractionListeners, with friendly fire ruled out by sensor groups and masks rather than an if-statement; kegs detonate with a real radial impulse, and flyers have their own collision group. In 3D it is a grassy plateau floating in mist, with a jointed low-poly rig per troop type.

Complete gameBlade Waltz

A Tales-style action-battle gauntlet on a round arena: ten waves of monsters, a party of four, and you control the swordsman only. Run freely, land 3-hit combos (J / click), spend TP on artes (K — neutral for the Demon Fang ground wave, toward the target for the Sonic Thrust dash, away for the launching Tiger Blade), guard with L, and unleash the Mystic Arte with SPACE once the Overlimit gauge fills from dealing and taking hits. Normal hits refill TP; guards only cover your front; stone golems and bosses shrug off single hits. Your AI party plays its roles: the lancer brawls and chains thrusts, the archer kites and volleys, the sage heals, revives and casts fire and stone with interruptible cast times. The tenth wave is a drake with claws, a tail spin, fire breath, a roar and wisp summons. Every combatant is a dynamic puck inside a ring of static wall segments; melee is resolved from arcs, while arrows, bolts, fireballs and waves are sensor bodies delivered by InteractionListeners with side filtering done by sensor masks. In 3D a side-on chase camera frames the player–target line over a stone arena in a meadow, with jointed low-poly rigs for every combatant.

Complete gameShard Rush

3v3 top-down hero arena. A mine in the middle coughs up a shard every few seconds — carry 10 between your team and survive a 15-second countdown to win. Die and you drop everything you hold. Complete gameRaft Rapids

Build-a-raft passenger run on real fluid buoyancy. In the loading bay, click to place floating barrels, deck nodes and wind-catching sails, and drag part-to-part to connect them with springy rods. Hit Launch: the raft splashes in, the crane lowers a ragdoll passenger who is denser than water — overboard he goes under and the run is lost — and the current carries everything down a four-screen river: a staircase of three waterfalls, each taller than the last, with boulder slaloms and accelerating headraces between them, to the delivery dock. The passenger holds on with a breakable joint: slalom bumps he rides out, a big drop tears his grip and he tumbles — and if the current washes the raft back under him, he grabs hold again. Rods are soft DistanceJoints with live strain that snap on hard rock hits, and sails pull toward a gusty tailwind while they stay dry. Once you launch, the run is hands-off: the raft you designed either makes it or it doesn't. Space launches, 1–4 pick tools, R resets.

Complete gameKickoff

2v2 top-down arena soccer. You and an AI teammate (blue) take on two AI opponents on a walled pitch with recessed goal pockets and round posts. Walk into a slow ball to carry it at your feet; a kick (SPACE/E) fires it the way you're facing, and an opponent overlapping your carried ball can tackle it away — a full-speed shot is too fast to catch. Move with WASD/arrows, hold SHIFT to sprint on a stamina tank that drains in seconds and refills slowly (drain it fully and you're winded) — or on any device hold the pointer to steer (push it far to sprint) and quick-tap to kick. The AI pairs split into attacker and defender roles dynamically, dribble for the corners, make supporting runs, and manage their own stamina. First to 3 goals inside the 2-minute clock — a tie at full time goes to golden goal.

Complete gameCrash Test Hero

Turbo-Dismount-style crash scoring. Pose the ragdoll dummy on its cart by dragging any body part, then hold Space (or the LAUNCH button) to charge the ping-ponging power meter and release to launch it down the ramp, over the pit, down the staircase and into the trampoline, the spinning paddle wheel and the crate wall. Damage IS the score — every hard contact pays out points from measured totalContactsImpulse (head hits ×3), and the seatbelt, neck, shoulders and hips are real breakable constraints (maxForce + breakUnderForce) that snap for bonus points via a ConstraintListener(BREAK). Big hits freeze time and shake the camera. On the way down time nearly freezes — press Space again to eject the dummy at the fast-sweeping aim arrow's angle, FlatOut-style, into the obstacle gauntlet: bowling pins, wrecking balls, a field of spinning rotors only a good arc can reach, a seesaw and the crate wall. After ejecting, Space panic-flails (3 charges). R restarts.

Complete gameRagdoll Royale

Battle-royale spectator sport with 50 numbered ragdolls standing to attention across five decks. The world holds its breath until you make your one decision (click a dummy to bet on it, Space picks at random) — then a seeded hazard schedule eats the arena: telegraphed geyser blasts, ball cannons that ride up and down the walls, rockfall from telegraphed ceiling hatches, platforms yanked from under everyone, kinematic rotors that reverse at random while riding their shafts, and a floor that crumbles in random order into the void. Damage is measured, not scripted — hard contacts bruise via totalContactsImpulse, necks/shoulders/hips are breakable constraints that tear off under violent yanks, and 0% is a KO. Last dummy in play wins; the banner grades your bet by placement. Per-rig InteractionGroup keeps 500 ragdoll parts colliding with each other but never with themselves. Every round reseeds — R restarts.

Complete gameEscape Run

Homage to the Warcraft 3 escape map genre (Run Kitty Run and friends): one touch kills, checkpoints are circles of power, deaths are unlimited — only the clock judges you. The exit gate takes three key shards, one stashed off the fast line in each hazard section, so you cannot run the loop just once. Weave the counter-timed sweeps of the Patrol Hall while a stalker paces its floor, sprint the timed portcullises of the Gate Run between punch-up spikes and flagstones that crumble if you hesitate on them, drift the frictionless Frozen Ascent, then thread Crusher Row, where kinematic pistons shove you into lava — or flatten you outright if you let one close on you. Move with WASD/arrows — or hold the pointer to steer on any device. R restarts the run. Patrols are sensor-only kinematic bodies on analytic paths, gates and spikes toggle in and out of the Space, pistons push with real kinematic contacts, hunters steer at you inside an aggro ring but are slower than you, and the ice is just a lower steering lerp — one InteractionListener per game rule.

Complete gameCapture the Flag

3v3 top-down capture the flag. Run into the enemy flag to steal it, then carry it back into your own base circle — but a capture only counts while your own flag sits at home, so someone has to defend. A dash (SPACE/E, or a quick tap) is both tackle and escape: dashing into an opponent staggers them with a real momentum shove and always knocks a carried flag loose; a dropped flag skids, blinks, and walks home after 10s unless someone claims it — your own team's touch returns it instantly. Mid-field cover blocks turn straight chases into cornering duels. Move with WASD/arrows, hold SHIFT to sprint on a stamina tank that drains in seconds and refills slowly (drain it fully and you're winded; flag carriers run slightly heavy) — or on any device hold the pointer to steer (push it far to sprint) and tap to dash. Each AI rolls its own speed and aggression and plays a live role — raider, mid-field interceptor, or goalkeeper-style flag guard — chasing carriers, escorting yours home, and racing loose flags. First to 3 captures inside the 2:30 clock; a tie at full time goes to sudden death, where even the guards abandon post and raid.

Complete gameDodgeball

3v3 top-down dodgeball, best of 3 rounds. The center line is a wall only for players — a collision-filtered fence pucks can't cross but balls fly straight over. Four balls start on the line: rush, walk into one to pick it up, carry it a beat to arm it (the classic fall-back-before-you-throw rule), then throw — SPACE/E auto-aims the nearest red, a tap throws exactly where you tap. A thrown ball is live (it wears the thrower's color) until it touches a wall or slows down; a live ball that touches an opponent knocks them out. There is no catching — when a ball comes at you, dodge. The court bleeds ammo too: every 20s one loose ball drains away for good (it blinks first), down to a single ball. Move with WASD/arrows, hold SHIFT to sprint on a stamina tank that drains in seconds and refills slowly — or on any device hold the pointer to steer (push it far to sprint) and quick-tap to throw. Every AI rolls its own reaction distance, lead-aim skill, throw scatter and aggression, so reds dodge your throws sideways off the flight line and lead yours. Stall too long and at 45s the line drops — the fence body leaves the space and the round turns into a point-blank melee. Last team standing takes the round; first to 2 rounds wins.

Complete gameStandoff

A top-down stand-still-to-shoot roguelite. Your hero auto-fires at the nearest foe only while standing still — the instant you move, the weapon goes cold, so each fight is stop-shoot-dodge. Clear every enemy in a room and draft one of three permanent perks that stack for the whole run: extra arrow directions (front, side, rear, diagonal), routing (piercing, wall ricochet, enemy-to-enemy ricochet), payload (explosive shot, bolt chain lightning, freeze, poison), companions (spirit orbs), and stats (crit, damage, speed, bloodthirst, quickdraw). Cleared rooms open a random-wall exit door; walking into it fades to the next arena. Melee rush with charges, ranged kite and snipe, dashers blink in, and every 5th room is a boss with shotguns and bullet rings. Deeper rooms add active hazards: telegraphed spike traps, lava pools, and spinning sawblades (kinematic bars that sweep the arena and block shots). Fallen enemies sometimes drop a healing heart — walk over it while wounded to patch up. Move with WASD / arrows or the bottom-left virtual stick. Camera shake on every kill, hit, trap, and boss slam.

Complete gameArena Defense

Mash-up of top-down shooter + tower defense + portals + destructible bodies. Big 1800×1200 world with a camera that follows the player. Complete gameContraption Garage

Fantastic-Contraption-style vehicle builder. In the build zone, click to place powered wheels (drive right / drive left) and frame nodes, and drag part-to-part to connect them with springy rods. Hit Test: rods become soft DistanceJoints, each wheel drives via a MotorJoint against its frame, and the camera follows your machine down a three-screen course — pit, whoop bumps, a gap to bridge, a stair climb and a cliff chute. Reaching the flag yourself wins nothing: a pink cargo ball waits on the high plateau, and IT has to get to the flag. Driven rims kick the ball away with backspin, so a bare car just tramples it — try a reverse-spinning wheel as an intake roller, and bring enough traction to push the cargo up the final ramp. Rods show live strain and snap when overloaded, mid-test you can grab parts with a springy pivot hand, and parts overlap freely thanks to InteractionFilter groups. Space toggles test, 1–4 pick tools, R resets.

Gameplay sliceSumo Arena

10-player free-for-all knock-out on a floating circular platform — shove everyone else off and be the last one standing. You control the blue puck against 9 AI fighters with edge-awareness, target picking and their own dash timing. Gameplay sliceCargo Crane Captain

Load ten crates onto a barge that actually floats — a fluid-enabled water body, not a static platform. Gameplay sliceSticky Builder

Physics construction game. Drag a spare blob near the truss and release to weld it in with springy DistanceJoint struts (soft constraints — stiff=false). Bridge the wide chasm — a mid-span rock pillar helps — and reach the beacon above the far platform before the spare blobs run out; on connect the survivors march home along the struts. Struts show live strain and snap when overstretched — yank a welded node (springy PivotJoint hand) to tear the truss apart and reclaim its blob. The seed's feet are pinned with PivotJoint anchors, and blobs never collide with each other thanks to InteractionFilter groups.
